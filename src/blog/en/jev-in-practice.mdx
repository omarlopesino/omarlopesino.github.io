## What Jev is

Explain what Jev is, why it generates no text and what it returns.

- TypeSafe's decision model
- Generates no text
- Calibrated probabilities
- RLCD: calibrated decisions
- `jev-latest` and `jev-preview` aliases

## Why Jev is practical

Explain what Jev offers over a generative LLM when making decisions.

- Structured answers, no parsing
- Predictable, verifiable output
- You only pay input
- Several questions, one request

## Question types, with an example of each

Present the four question types with a generic request and response example.

- `noul`: yes or no
- `choice`: one option among many
- `score`: ordered levels
- `box`: where something is
- Best practices when asking

## Reading the answer and deciding

Explain how to turn probabilities into stored values and choose sensible thresholds.

- Probabilities, not closed answers
- Thresholds tuned with examples
- Read `confidence` too
- When to defer to humans

Tell where Jev is used today, starting with its Drupal integration.

- The `ai_provider_typesafeai` provider
- AI Automators Decision rules
- Text classification
