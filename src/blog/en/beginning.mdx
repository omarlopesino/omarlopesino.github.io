Welcome to the first post of the website! This is the end of a journey where the shape,
purpose and stack of the website have been built.  Follow me in this post which will explain all the steps involved in the website's creation process. I will start explaining why I did it, the background before coming to the idea, the decisions driven by all my experience as a web developer, and the technical details around the page building.

## The purpose

I sometimes feel I want to express my technical learnings and thoughts. As I work I am constantly learning as step by step I am getting a Computer Science engineering graduate.
All that learnings make my vision about the IT world broadly. Just now, we are experiencing the rising of AI that is reshaping the way we are working, and is likely to stay
forever. 

Rising of AI makes me dedicate time to self learn how AI works: for development and to make apps that rely on non-deterministic agents. All these learnings have motivated me to have a place to show my articles. Of course, Generative AI is not everything.
I would like to talk about more topics as machine learning, some applications I am doing. Also I plan to share some experiences as future events like Drupal camp 2026, Drupal Dev Days 2027 and incoming events.
There is a lot of endless topics that can I can express in a site.

Also, the rising of AI has also produced a lot of internet content to be generated by AI. So this is a way to make internet content still human.

So, the main purpose is having a page that serves as my personal development blog. Other purposes cover having a way to expose my networks , my work, and a brief presentation about me.

To make this purpose possible, I need to make choices based on my previous experience. Specifically, the experience of making years earlier a personal website.

## A bit of history

This is the second time I make a website. The first time I launched a website was [in 2018](https://github.com/omarlopesino/omarlopesino-nuxt-legacy).
It was a basic portfolio made with Nuxt and Drupal. My idea was making a decoupled site where all the content was created with [Contenta CMS](https://www.contentacms.org/). It worked fine. Even it had an
automated [travis ci job](https://github.com/omarlopesino/omarlopesino-nuxt-legacy/blob/master/.travis.yml) that would automatically publish the site on github pages. I unpublished
the page as I felt it was unfinished. I've also felt that doesn't matter every automation I add, maintaining a decoupled site might consume a lot of my personal time. It involves maintaining the backend: PHP version and packages, Drupal upgrades,
contenta CMS compatibility, and unexpected issues. And also the npm stack. That made me unconciously make a decision: if I want to have a personal website I want it to be extremely simple.

## The decisions

### Keeping it simple
Deciding to make the website as simple as possible has been a blind decision I made. I did it passively through the building of the website. I want to have a page that makes me
adding new content easy. I don't want complicated features and processes to maintain the site. 

### It will be a static website made in Astro

With custom I mean crafting the page by myself, with the help of frameworks. Astro provides me tools to create my custom theme. Also, it lets me no bother on security issues, as once I publish content, all it contais is harmless HTML.
It also allows me writing in Markdown with is pretty easy. There are better options for creating content such as robust CMS as Drupal, but static websites let's me not being forced to host my page in a server.

### A personal website does not be sophisticated
It was not only the search, the full stack of a website came to my mind. FOr me, a website has search, cookie compliance, analytics, post subscription, comments, etc. Then I thought ¿DO I really want that?
My purpose is just publishing stuff. I want to collect nobody's information, neither mails or their activity. I personally dislike telemetry, besides it is hard to do it every time. Of course I want other's feedback, but that can be done through social networks.

### The page must be focused on blog posts
Of course, what I wanted the people to do at my page? I want them to read my posts, but also read about me. I don't pretend to provide a curriculum in my web, I have a more elaborate one if someone asks me for.
That is why the homepage is just a brief introduction that let's you see the categories and recent posts. For a more detailed presentation, I have the about me page.

### A styleguide will help a backend to design

I am an expert backend developer. My frontend experience is much smaller. So, when it comes to design components and layouts of the site, the creation becomes a bit daunting. As I am an analytical person,
I work better under a structured way to develop. That is why I started making the site with Storybook, which allows me to create every component before stepping into creatively creating every page.
This has been my favorite part to develop. I could learn a lot of modern design practices, component development, and tailwind. And a lot of css features that are new to me like the [View Transition API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/View_Transition_API)

### The search must be simple
First I thought that it was a good idea to add a complex faceted search. Then I noted that probably the amount of posts I will do doesn't need that. A sophisticated search may be too complex. And I want to do something simple. That is why to search content in the site will be possible just
by browsing categories, tags and archive.

### The beginning of development must be coded by me

This post is written in 2026. Generative AI is on demand. Coding with AI has become . It has become game changing, everything has changed after the Generative AI had become usable. 
Also, very polemic. I will deep about it in upcoming posts.

As I developer I think my personal website should be handcrafted to show my abilities. But also, showing that I can use AI to build the page shows my ability to agilize work with the latest technologies.
Also, I want to make the development processs simple, every decisions turns around taking it easy. Delegating all the process to think every line of code, fixing bugs, etc, is easier if it is automated by AI.

That is why I've chosen to mix efforts. Most of the time spent on this page has been me coding most of the components and pages, including all its HTML, CSS and JS.
Once I noted I have built enough parts of the site, I am relying on AI. This makes it follows easier my conventions, skeleton and way to code.  Also, it is intuitive for me knowing
how every part is done. And it is not a code external to me, it has a lot of my contributions. If I run out of a coding subscription, it will incredibly help continuing myself.

### Posts will be fully written by me

This breaks my "making it simple rule". But, in moments that it is possible to automate every online automation , from writing to streaming, it is important to push towards to 
human interaction. 

## Conclusions
This page may not be so important or relevant, but it has a meaning for me. It is very satisfactory to 
finish a project as small as it is. Also, having a space to express myself will be of big gratitude.
