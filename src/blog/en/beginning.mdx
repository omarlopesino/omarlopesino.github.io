Welcome to the first post of the website! This marks the end of the journey of getting a first version of my site ready to go. In this post I want to cover the purpose of the site, the decisions I made building it, and what it means to me.

## The purpose

I would like to have a space to express my technical learnings and thoughts. I have a lot of learnings from the current Computer Science career I am studying now, my professional experience, and readings / personal projects. Additionally, the rising of Generative AI is a great
source of fresh knowledge.

The current development of Generative AI makes me dedicate time to self-learning how it works, especially for coding and apps that are using AI.
I would like to talk also about more topics such as machine learning and some applications I am doing. Also, I plan to share some experiences from future events like [Drupal Camp Spain 2026](https://2026.drupalcamp.es/es).

In short, the main purpose of my personal page is to write technical posts. Other purposes cover having a way to promote ways to contact me, my work, and a brief presentation about me. Last, but not least, this helps enrich the internet with human content, as I plan to write the posts myself, countering the [Dead Internet Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dead_Internet_theory).

## A bit of history

Before digging into decisions, I would like to talk about my previous experience making a personal website.

The first time I launched a website was [in 2018](https://github.com/omarlopesino/omarlopesino-nuxt-legacy). It was a basic portfolio made with Nuxt and Drupal. My idea was making a decoupled site where all the content was created with [Contenta CMS](https://www.contentacms.org/). It worked fine. Even it had an
automated [Travis CI job](https://github.com/omarlopesino/omarlopesino-nuxt-legacy/blob/master/.travis.yml) that would automatically publish the site on GitHub Pages. I unpublished
the page as I felt it was unfinished. I've also felt that no matter how much automation I add, maintaining a decoupled site might consume a lot of my personal time. It involves maintaining the backend: PHP version and packages, Drupal upgrades,
Contenta CMS compatibility, and unexpected issues. That made me unconsciously make a decision: if I want to have a personal website I want it to be extremely simple.

## The decisions

With this purpose in mind and my previous background, these are the most important decisions I made to bring the website to life.

### Keeping it simple

This is a decision I made unconsciously, as I want to take it easy: no complicated features, and a light maintenance process. One example of this is the search.
First I thought that it was a good idea to add a complex faceted search. Then I noted that the number of posts is manageable. By a simple clustering of the content by categories, tags and years, I ensure that every post won't get lost over time.

### A personal website does not need to be sophisticated

It was not only the search; the full stack of a website came to my mind in order to make it simple. For me, a website has search, cookie compliance, analytics, post subscription, comments, etc. Then I thought: do I really want that?
My purpose is just publishing stuff. I want to collect nobody's information, neither emails nor their activity. I personally dislike telemetry; besides, it is hard to do it every time. Of course I want others' feedback, but that can be done with different methods.

### The website will be made in Astro

Astro can generate static HTML pages. It does not need a server to run. I must continue maintaining the packages used for development, but that is much lighter. I must only check that my production infrastructure, Cloudflare and GitHub Pages, are up.
It also allows me to create a theme from scratch. And, the most important: creating content is pretty quick thanks to them being written in Markdown. It is true that I don't have
the sophisticated tools that provide a CMS like Drupal or WordPress, but is enough for me.

### The page will be focused on blog posts

Of course, what did I want people to do on my page? I want them to read my posts, but also read about me. I don't pretend to provide a curriculum on my website; I have a more elaborate one if someone asks for it.
That is why the homepage is just a brief introduction that lets you see the categories and recent posts. For a more detailed presentation, I have the about me page.

### A styleguide is the ground to develop the entire website

When it comes to design components and layouts of the site, the creation becomes a bit daunting for me. As I am an analytical person,
I work better under a structured way to develop. That is why I started making the site with Storybook, which allows me to create every component before stepping into creatively creating every page.
This has been my favorite part to develop. This has let me learn a lot of modern design practices, component development, and Tailwind. And a lot of CSS features that are new to me like the [View Transition API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/View_Transition_API).

It also allows decomposing all the website components easily, without needing to look at the big picture every time.

### The beginning of development must be coded by me

Even though AI is able to code the entire website from scratch, I think it is more responsible to start by myself and then let the AI do the work. In this way, I can learn from scratch how Astro works, how to use all daisyUI and Tailwind classes, and how
to build a storybook. After building most of the components and pages, I've just let the AI continue the work to provide the end result.

### Posts will be mostly written by me

This breaks my "making it simple rule". But, in times when it is possible to automate every online automation, from writing to streaming, it is important to push toward
human interaction. My process implies first writing the content by myself. Then, I plan to use AI to review my writings and provide detailed continuous feedback.

## The meaning

This site has a meaning for me, independent of its content or how many people it attracts. It is very satisfactory to 
finish a project as small as it is. There are too many ideas and personal projects that never get finished due to eternal delays, dealing with real-life problems,
or just rejecting the idea that looked great at the beginning.

Having a space to express myself is very satisfactory. I can express my opinions which may connect with some reader. Also, my knowledge can
help others dive into new concepts, either to get a grasp or understand how something works. And, I can provide fresh content to the internet, pushing back against soul-less AI content.

Let's see how this personal project goes over time, how much knowledge and how many opinions I can share, and what influence they have.

Thanks for reading.
